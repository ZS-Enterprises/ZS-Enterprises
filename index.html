About Us

Precision in every detail.
Craft in every corner.

ZS Enterprises delivers fabrication and interior solutions for residential, commercial and architectural projects — working across MS, stainless steel, aluminium and wood.

We pair practical engineering with a design-conscious eye, coordinating measurements, materials and finishing across every material so the project moves forward without friction — built for builders, architects, interior designers and studios who need one dependable fabrication partner.

  • ◆ Custom fabrication engineered to site & design requirements
  • ◆ Close attention to measurements, alignment & finishing
  • ◆ Trusted by architects, designers, contractors & homeowners
  • ◆ One-point coordination across MS, SS, aluminium & wood
